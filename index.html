Common questions

Clear answers before you book.

How fast can I get an estimate?

For many small repairs, the fastest path is to text 2 to 3 clear photos, a short description, and your service ZIP code to 623-227-8884. The admin desk will triage your request and respond with next steps.

Do I need to be home?

For occupied homes, we coordinate access and arrival expectations in advance. For rentals or investor properties, photo documentation can help simplify the process.

What work is outside Prime’s standard scope?

Prime focuses on itemized repairs, maintenance, upgrades, and direct like-for-like replacements where permitted. Major structural additions, full home rewires, large mainline plumbing overhauls, and permit-required work are outside standard handyman scope.

What does the workmanship warranty cover?

The Prime Workmanship Warranty is a standard 30-day labor-only warranty on completed work. Materials, customer-supplied parts, pre-existing damage, and unrelated failures are not included.
