Pricing FAQ

Common questions, straight answers.

Is there a "trip charge" on top of the service call?

No. The $139 Prime Service Call includes the first hour of labor — the trip is built in. What you approve is what you pay.

Do you give free estimates?

Simple jobs get a flat-rate or text-based estimate up front. Larger or complex work may require a short site visit, which is covered by the $139 service call if you proceed.

What if the job ends up bigger than expected?

We stop, show you the issue, and give you a written change order with the cost before continuing. You always approve scope changes first.

Do I pay for materials separately?

Materials are passed through at cost and itemized on your invoice. For jobs over $400, a 50% deposit covers upfront material costs.

What forms of payment do you accept?

Credit/debit cards, cash, and checks. We'll confirm what works best when we book your job.
