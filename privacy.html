Privacy Policy

Your information stays with Prime.

Last updated: January 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you request an estimate, call, or text us, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, service ZIP code, and a description of your project. If you send photos of your project, we use them only to provide your estimate.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to your request, provide estimates, schedule and perform services, and communicate with you about your project. We do not sell your personal information.

3. How We Store Your Information

Estimate requests are stored securely and accessed only by Prime Handyman Service LLC team members who need them to serve you. We retain request records only as long as needed for business and legal purposes.

4. Sharing

We do not rent or sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers (such as secure form processing) who are contractually bound to protect it, or when required by law.

5. Third-Party Services

Our estimate form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to protect against automated abuse, and may use Supabase to store form submissions. These services process data according to their own privacy policies.

6. Your Choices

You may ask us to review, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by contacting us at bryan.lewis@prime-handyman-az.com or calling 623-227-8884.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date.
